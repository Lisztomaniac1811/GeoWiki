# Source Desk Reading Room

Reading Room is the editable dossier phase after link collection and triage. It keeps the project self-contained while preserving the original source URL as a compact external reference.

## Best way to open it

Open `index.html`, load your research case, then choose **Reading room**. Keep the Source Desk tab open for live two-way synchronization.

Reading Room receives:

- every source filed in the chronology;
- saved source text, notes, quotes, tags, quality, relevance, and dates;
- locally stored image, video, audio, PDF, and document attachments available in that browser.

## Editing a dossier entry

Choose **Edit dossier entry** to change its title, type, date, date confidence, source quality, relevance, tags, research notes, pull quote, saved article/document text, and primary attached media.

- **Return to link mess** demotes the source out of the Reading Room chronology.
- **Delete** permanently removes it from the current case after confirmation.
- When opened from Source Desk, these changes synchronize back immediately.
- When opened independently, changes persist in the Reading Room cache; use **Save dossier** to download a portable copy.

## Self-contained reading

Articles, PDFs, documents, social sources, and other records can carry a saved reading copy. Paste only the passages you need or a full cleaned article. Saved text is searchable, included in writing packets, and exported to Markdown and case files.

For video sources, a locally attached video is preferred as the primary player. YouTube or Vimeo remains an optional fallback, and the original URL remains available as a compact link.

## Views

- **Chronological reading** — edit and read complete dossier entries in date order.
- **Media wall** — browse attached images/videos and supported video URLs.
- **Writing desk** — build a pinned evidence packet from saved text, notes, quotes, and the private scratchpad.

Reviewed markers, writing pins, and the scratchpad remain reader-specific browser metadata. Source edits are part of the project itself.
